# Purpose

This document maps the end-to-end experience of a conference attendee using ConCrew.

The objective is to understand the user's goals, thoughts, emotions, pain points, and opportunities throughout the conference journey.

Rather than focusing only on application features, this document focuses on the user's overall experience.

---

# User Persona

## Alex

**Occupation**

Software Engineer

**Conference Experience**

First-time attendee

**Primary Goal**

Meet compatible people before arriving at the conference and experience the event as part of a small community.

---

# Journey Overview

```text
Plan Conference
        ↓
Join ConCrew
        ↓
Discover Compatible People
        ↓
Form a Crew
        ↓
Experience Conference Together
        ↓
Maintain Professional Relationships
```

---

# Stage 1 — Planning

**Timeline**

2–4 Weeks Before the Conference

---

## User Goals

- Purchase conference ticket
- Book flights and accommodation
- Plan conference schedule
- Decide which sessions to attend
- Find people to experience the conference with

---

## User Thoughts

> "I hope I meet interesting people."

> "I don't know anyone attending."

> "I don't want to spend three days alone."

---

## User Actions

- Purchases conference ticket
- Books hotel
- Books flight
- Downloads conference application
- Discovers ConCrew

---

## Pain Points

- Doesn't know anyone attending
- Unsure how to begin networking
- Feels uncertain about attending alone

---

## Opportunity

ConCrew introduces attendees before they arrive.

Instead of arriving alone, users begin building relationships before the conference starts.

---

# Stage 2 — Join the Community

**Timeline**

1–2 Weeks Before the Conference

---

## User Goals

- Meet attendees before arriving
- Build confidence
- Find compatible people

---

## User Actions

- Creates ConCrew account
- Builds professional profile
- Completes conference preferences
- Joins conference
- Selects interests
- Defines networking preferences

---

## User Thoughts

> "I wonder who else is attending."

> "Hopefully I can find people with similar interests."

---

## Pain Points

- Doesn't know who to connect with
- Doesn't want to send random connection requests

---

## Opportunity

ConCrew recommends attendees based on compatibility rather than requiring users to browse hundreds of profiles.

---

# Stage 3 — Discover Your Crew

**Timeline**

Several Days Before the Conference

---

## User Goals

- Build a small conference crew
- Meet people before arrival
- Feel comfortable before day one

---

## User Actions

- Views recommendations
- Explores attendee profiles
- Reviews compatibility explanations
- Sends connection requests
- Joins conversations
- Forms a crew

---

## User Thoughts

> "These people seem like a great fit."

> "I'm actually excited to meet them."

---

## Success Criteria

The user arrives at the conference already knowing several attendees.

---

# Stage 4 — Experience the Conference

**Timeline**

Conference Days

---

## User Goals

- Meet crew members
- Attend sessions
- Discuss presentations
- Network naturally

---

## User Actions

- Meets crew members
- Attends presentations together
- Eats lunch together
- Visits exhibitors
- Continues technical discussions
- Introduces one another to new people

---

## User Thoughts

> "This conference feels much more enjoyable."

> "I'm learning more because I have people to discuss ideas with."

---

## Pain Points Without ConCrew

- Attending sessions alone
- Feeling uncomfortable approaching strangers
- Missing opportunities to continue conversations
- Difficulty finding compatible attendees

---

## Opportunity

ConCrew transforms networking from random encounters into intentional relationships.

---

# Stage 5 — Continue the Relationship

# Key UX Opportunities

The journey identifies several opportunities where ConCrew provides value.

## Before the Conference

Reduce uncertainty by helping attendees discover compatible people.

---

## During the Conference

Help attendees continue discussions instead of ending conversations after sessions.

---

## After the Conference

Transform temporary conference interactions into long-term professional relationships.

---

# Key Product Insight

The research suggests attendees are not simply looking to network.

They are looking for:

- Shared experiences
- Meaningful conversations
- Compatible people
- A sense of belonging

Networking becomes the outcome rather than the primary objective.

---

# Success Definition

The user journey is considered successful when:

- The attendee arrives already knowing compatible people.
- Networking feels natural instead of intimidating.
- The attendee experiences the conference as part of a small community.
- Relationships continue after the conference.
