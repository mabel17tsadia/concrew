# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to understand the experiences, motivations, frustrations, and behaviors of conference attendees.

Rather than validating a predetermined solution, this research aims to determine whether a meaningful problem exists and whether ConCrew addresses that problem.

The findings from this phase will influence product decisions, feature prioritization, and future iterations of the MVP.

---

# Research Background

The idea for ConCrew originated after attending **AI Engineers World Fair** in San Francisco.

Although the conference delivered exceptional technical content, one aspect of the experience felt incomplete.

After attending presentations and workshops, there were many interesting ideas worth discussing, yet there was no established group of people to continue those conversations with.

Thousands of professionals attended the conference, but many appeared to experience it independently.

This raised a simple question:

> **How can conference attendees discover compatible people before arriving at an event?**

# Initial Observations

The following observations are based on firsthand experience attending AI Engineers World Fair.

---

# Why I Attended

My reasons for attending included:

- Experience my first major technology conference.
- Learn about current developments in Artificial Intelligence.
- Identify valuable technical skills for future career growth.
- Explore internship and employment opportunities.
- Meet professionals working in AI.
- Expand my professional network.
- Experience a new city.
- Leave inspired and motivated.

---

# Challenges Observed

## Information Overload

The conference offered dozens of sessions occurring simultaneously.

Some sessions required premium tickets, forcing attendees to quickly adjust their schedules and choose alternative presentations.

As a result:

- Choosing sessions became stressful.
- It was difficult to determine which talks provided the most value.
- Attendees constantly worried about missing something important.

---

## Limited Time to Process

Conference schedules were extremely busy.

After attending a presentation, attendees often had to immediately move to another session.

This left little opportunity to:

- Reflect on new ideas.
- Ask deeper questions.
- Discuss concepts with others.
- Apply what had just been learned.

---

## Difficult Networking

Although thousands of professionals attended the conference, meaningful conversations rarely happened naturally.

Many attendees were:

- Walking between sessions
- Looking for food
- Exploring exhibitors
- Taking personal breaks
- Preparing for the next presentation

Approaching strangers often felt awkward because everyone appeared busy.

---

## Existing Conference Applications

The official conference application successfully provided:

- Session schedules
- Speaker information
- Exhibitor listings
- Attendee directory

However, it did not help attendees answer questions such as:

- Who shares my interests?
- Who works in a similar role?
- Who attended the same session?
- Who is attending alone?
- Who wants to continue discussing today's presentations?

The application answered:

> **Who is attending?**

It did not answer:

> **Who should I meet?**

# Product Hypotheses

The following hypotheses were developed from these initial observations.

---

## Hypothesis 1

Conference attendees want to connect before arriving at an event.

---

## Hypothesis 2

Attendees prefer personalized recommendations over manually browsing hundreds of profiles.

---

## Hypothesis 3

Small networking crews (3–5 people) create more meaningful relationships than large communities.

---

## Hypothesis 4

Compatibility should consider both professional background **and** conference behavior.

Potential compatibility factors include:

### Professional

- Company
- School
- Job title
- Industry
- Experience level

### Conference

- Goals
- Session interests
- Networking preferences
- First-time attendee status

---

## Hypothesis 5

Attendees are looking for people to experience conferences with—not simply collect professional connections.

# Key Insight

The most important insight discovered during product discovery is:

> **Conference attendees are not simply looking to network.**

They are looking to discover compatible people they can experience the conference with.

This insight fundamentally changed the direction of the product.

Instead of becoming another networking platform, ConCrew aims to become a conference companion that helps attendees form meaningful conference crews before arriving at an event.
