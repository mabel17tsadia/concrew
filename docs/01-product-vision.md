# ConCrew

> **Find your conference crew before you arrive.**

ConCrew is a conference networking platform that helps attendees discover compatible people **before**, **during**, and **after** professional conferences.

Rather than relying on chance encounters, attendees can build meaningful relationships before arriving at the venue, making networking feel natural instead of intimidating.

---

# The Problem

Professional conferences are designed to connect people.

Ironically, many attendees experience conferences alone.

Although thousands of people with similar interests gather in one place, meaningful networking often depends on luck.

Conference attendees frequently experience:

- Information overload
- Difficulty approaching strangers
- Limited time between sessions
- Missed networking opportunities
- No structured way to continue conversations after workshops

Existing conference applications answer questions like:

- Who is speaking?
- Which sessions are available?
- Where are the exhibitors?

Very few answer the question that matters most:

> **"Who should I spend this conference with?"**

---

# Product Opportunity

ConCrew exists to reduce the uncertainty of conference networking.

Instead of asking users to browse hundreds of attendee profiles, the platform helps them discover people they are most likely to enjoy meeting.

Recommendations are based on compatibility rather than popularity.

Potential compatibility factors include:

- Professional background
- Technical interests
- Conference goals
- Networking preferences
- Shared sessions
- University
- Company
- Location

The objective is not to help users collect hundreds of connections.

The objective is to help users find **their conference crew**.

---

# Background

The inspiration for ConCrew came from attending **AI Engineers World Fair** in San Francisco.

The conference itself was exceptional.

I attended to:

- Experience my first major AI conference
- Learn what was happening across the AI industry
- Better understand which technical skills to develop
- Explore internship and career opportunities
- Meet professionals working in AI
- Experience a new city
- Leave inspired and motivated

The technical sessions exceeded expectations.

However, one aspect of the experience felt incomplete.

After attending presentations, there were countless ideas worth discussing, yet there was no established group of people to continue those conversations with.

Although thousands of software engineers, researchers, founders, and students attended the conference, many appeared to be navigating the event independently.

This raised one simple question:

> **What if attendees could discover compatible people before arriving at the conference?**

Instead of spending the first day wondering who to talk to, attendees could arrive already knowing a small group of people who shared similar interests and goals.

---

# Vision

> **To create a platform where attending a conference alone never means experiencing it alone.**

ConCrew aims to transform conference networking from random encounters into intentional communities.

The platform should make conferences:

- More collaborative
- Less intimidating
- More valuable
- More memorable

---

- Researchers

- Students

---

## Secondary Users

- First-time conference attendees
- Solo travelers
- University groups
- Coworkers attending the same event
- Professional organizations

---

# Value Proposition

ConCrew helps conference attendees discover the **right people** before arriving at an event.

Instead of relying on random networking opportunities, users can:

- Find compatible attendees
- Build meaningful professional relationships
- Form small conference crews
- Continue conversations beyond conference sessions

The platform prioritizes **quality of connections over quantity of connections.**

---

# Product Goals

The MVP focuses on validating one core hypothesis:

> Conference attendees will have a better conference experience if they can discover compatible people before arriving.

To achieve this, the product should:

- Help attendees connect before conferences.
- Reduce the anxiety of approaching strangers.
- Encourage meaningful post-session discussions.
- Promote small networking groups.
- Increase the value attendees gain from conferences.
- Encourage users to return for future conferences.

---

# Success Criteria (MVP)

The MVP will be considered successful if users can:

### Functional Success

- Create an account
- Complete their profile
- Join a conference
- Browse attendees
- Receive recommendations
- Send connection requests
- Create or join a crew

---

### User Success

Users should be able to:

- Discover at least one person they genuinely want to meet.
- Join or create a small networking crew.
- Participate in at least one meetup.
- Leave the conference with meaningful professional relationships.

---

# Non-Goals

The MVP intentionally excludes:

- Conference ticket sales
- Hotel and flight booking
- Video conferencing
- AI chatbots
- Large discussion forums
- Job boards
- Conference management
- Live streaming
- Payment processing

These features may be evaluated after validating the core networking experience.

---

# Product Principles

Every product decision should support one or more of the following principles.

## 1. Meaningful Connections Over Large Networks

Success is measured by relationship quality rather than connection count.

---

## 2. Small Communities Over Large Crowds

Networking should feel personal, not overwhelming.

---

## 3. Discovery Before Networking

Help users identify the right people before asking them to start conversations.

---

## 4. Real Conversations Over Endless Messaging

The platform exists to improve in-person interactions, not replace them.

---

## 5. Simplicity Wins

Every feature should solve a real user problem without adding unnecessary complexity.

---

# Summary

ConCrew began with a simple observation:

> Conferences bring together thousands of like-minded people, yet many attendees experience them alone.

By helping attendees discover compatible people before they arrive, ConCrew aims to transform conferences into shared experiences built around collaboration, learning, and meaningful professional relationships.
